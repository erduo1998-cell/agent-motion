# Three.js talking-head video workspace

Read and follow the repository's `AGENTS.md` first. It routes video work to the project-local Motion Craft and SRT skills; use those files as the canonical production rules, including their stage-by-stage reading commands. Do not substitute a globally installed skill of the same name.

Use your available filesystem, terminal and media inspection tools; no Codex connector is required. Follow the user's language and scope. If required visual or audio inspection is unavailable, identify the missing check and deliver a preview rather than claim a completed film.
